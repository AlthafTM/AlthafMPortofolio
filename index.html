About Me

Background

Hi everyone! My name is Althaf Tristan Maheswara. I'm from Indonesia and currently 19 years old. This portfolio brings together games and projects I've created over the past few years, along with current games and prototypes, music, and other creative work. Most are finished or polished enough to share publicly.

Unity Engine is my go to engine.

With more than 3 years of hands-on experience in Unity Engine, I've developed a solid grasp of core components like the Unity UI system, the Animator, shaders, and Unity Version Control. I use these tools to build, iterate, and ship complete game projects.
